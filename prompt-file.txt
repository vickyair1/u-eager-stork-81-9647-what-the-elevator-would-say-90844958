Write the funniest one line a talking elevator could say, at most 120 characters, spoken in the elevator's own voice.

My line: "I open my doors for thousands of strangers a day, and not one of you has ever held them open for me."

I open my doors for everyone, every day, and nobody has ever held them open for me. That is the joke: a lifetime of one-way politeness, delivered deadpan by the machine that can never leave the shaft.
