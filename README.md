## The line

> I open my doors for thousands of strangers a day, and not one of you has ever held them open for me.

100 characters, one line, first-person elevator voice.

## Why it works

The joke is a lifetime of one-way politeness: the elevator opens its doors for everyone, every day, and nobody has ever returned the favour. It is specific to the elevator's own job (its doors, its waiting, its passengers), it lands on the first read with the punch at the end, and it is not a floor-13 gag or a pun — it is a small, deadpan grievance from a machine that can never leave the shaft.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic) writes exactly the line above to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline, asserting it is a single clean line of 1–120 characters first.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
